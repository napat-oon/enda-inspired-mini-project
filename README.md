# enda-inspired-mini-project
A small game built with Agentic AI workflow using GitHub Copilot. Created purely for the sake of practicing Agentic AI, not vibe coding. All credits go to the original creators if there are references.

## Purpose
Who will use it: gamers or people who are interested in a small game or mini project (with the help of Agentic AI).
What will it help them do: playing a video game as a form of entertainment or as a practice challenge.

## Requirements
- R1: Enemy will occasionally make an opening for player to attack for a limited time at a designated space. The player must move their cursor to that space and press attack input in the given moment. A successful player attack will decrease the enemy's health. Failing to attack in the designated time and space will cause nothing to change.
- R2: Enemy will often attack the player after hinting where it will attack at a designated space. The player must move their cursor to that space and press & hold block input in the given moment. As long as the player is holding block input at the correct area when the enemy's attack lands, it will be a successful block. A successful block will retain the player's health, no damage is done. Failing to block when the enemy's attack lands will decrease the player's health.
- R3: The game ends when the player or the enemy loses all their health. Whoever is still alive at the end wins. Make sure that the enemy never exposes itself and attack at the same or too similar time as a boundary, to ensure that neither of them may lose at the same time or at an unfair state. When the game ends, the enemy stops attacking and any inputs will not cause any change to health to prevent unending game.
- R4: After the game ends in win or loss for the player, the player has the option to restart the game. Upon restart, the game state begins as if the game is opened the first time.
